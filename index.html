At Sri Nrityalaya dance is taught not just as a means of entertainment but as an offering to the supreme soul.

Founded by Srividya Anand in 2003 at Ramamurthy Nagar, Bangalore, Sri Nrityalaya offers training in Bharatanatyam in the widely known and respected Kalakshetra style. Close to 80 students are at various stages of their artistic pursuits at Sri Nrityalaya.

At Sri Nrityalaya students are taught performing arts in its truest and purest form. It is ensured that every student is strongly rooted in the fundamentals of Bharatanatyam. Senior students of Sri Nrityalaya have performed extensively in numerous events and prestigious dance festivals in Bangalore, Kerala and Tamil Nadu.

Recently students performed in Ayodhya as well as Shirdi Sai temple. Students of Sri Nrityalaya have appeared and passed with excellent grades in exams conducted by Gandharva Mahavidyalaya as well as Karnataka Board.

About our Director

Srividya Anand

Srividya Anand is a graduate in Bharatanatyam from the world renowned institution Kalakshetra Foundation, Chennai.

She is a leading Bharatanatyam dancer, teacher and choreographer in Bangalore with more than 23 years of experience in teaching.

She had her initial training in dance at Kolkata under Smt. Mohana Iyer. Deciding to delve deeper into Bharatanatyam she joined Kalakshetra, Chennai and came under the tutelage of many eminent Gurus during her stint there. She was further mentored by Guru Sri Sheejith krishna and in Abhinaya by Guru Smt. Bragha Bessell.

Srividya Anand has performed in almost all the sabhas in Kolkata. She has also had the fortune of being a part of the various dance dramas of Kalakshetra. She has given many performances in India, notable among them being a performance in front of the Home Minister of Karnataka.

She has performed at Guruvayur, Attukaal temple Trivandrum, Kollur Mookambiga temple, Udupi Krishna temple, Shirdi Sai temple, Ayodhya and Navratri festivals at Chennai as well.

Srividya is the founder director of Sri Nrityalaya Institute of Bharatanatyam, at Ramamurthy Nagar in Bangalore and has been offering training in traditional Kalakshetra style since 2003.
